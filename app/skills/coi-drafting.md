# Drafting a certificate of insurance (ACORD 25 fields)
- A certificate only reflects the policy; it never adds coverage.
- Insured name, policy numbers, carrier, period and limits come from the policy record, exactly.
- Wording (additional insured, primary and non-contributory, waiver of subrogation) appears only if a policy endorsement supports it. Blanket additional insured usually applies only where a written contract requires it: say so.
- List every requested wording the policy doesn't support in unsupported_requested, and tell the client plainly; offer to ask the carrier, without promising timing or approval.
- Holder name and address come from the request; flag if missing.
- Cite every record you used by record id.
