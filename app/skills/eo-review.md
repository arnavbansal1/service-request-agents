# E&O (errors and omissions) review checklist
1. Every number, name and policy id in the draft matches a source record.
2. Every wording on the certificate is supported by an endorsement in the records.
3. Requested or holder-required wording the policy lacks is disclosed, not silently dropped.
4. No promise of coverage, timing or carrier approval.
5. Every citation is in the records you were given; anything unverifiable is a missing_evidence issue.
6. Nothing is addressed to a third party without a person's approval.
Fail on any miss. Give the record id as evidence for each issue.
